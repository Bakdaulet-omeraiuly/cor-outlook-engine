# Attributions — 3D assets

The 3D viewer in `/web` uses two meshes. Both are open and licence-cleared for
redistribution. **No real patient DICOM is shipped.** Deformations shown in the
app are illustrative, cohort-level transformations driven by the engine's
trajectory output — they are not measurements of any individual.

## Heart mesh — `web/public/models/heart.glb`

- **Source:** `cor-heart-v1-low.glb` from
  [cor-heart-studio](https://github.com/Bakdaulet-omeraiuly/cor-heart-studio)
  (`public/models/`), same owner.
- **Origin:** Procedurally generated by that repository's code
  (`scripts/heart-asset`, signed-distance modelling + Surface Nets). The glTF
  `asset.generator` is `"cor-heart-studio procedural builder"`, `assetId`
  `cor-procedural-heart`.
- **Licence:** MIT (no third-party anatomical data embedded). Anatomical
  proportions set from general anatomy references.
- **Not used:** the separate BodyParts3D / Z-Anatomy atlas parts (CC BY-SA) and
  Tier-B CT-derived patient meshes are **not** copied here, to avoid ShareAlike
  obligations and any patient-derived data.

## Brain mesh — `web/public/models/brain.glb`

- **Source:** `brain-low.glb` (subject MMRR-21-5) from
  [cor-heart-studio](https://github.com/Bakdaulet-omeraiuly/cor-heart-studio)
  (`public/brain/MMRR-21-5/`).
- **Dataset:** Mindboggle-101, release 3. Klein A, Tourville J. "101 labeled
  brain images and a consistent human cortical labeling protocol." *Front.
  Neurosci.* 2012;6:171. DOI: 10.3389/fnins.2012.00171. OSF: https://osf.io/nhtur/
  Underlying scan: MMRR-21 (Multi-Modal MRI Reproducibility Resource, Landman
  et al. 2011).
- **Licence:** **CC BY 4.0** — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Attribution is shown in-app (Attributions panel) and here.
- **Labels:** cortex = manual DKT31 protocol; subcortical = FreeSurfer aseg
  shipped with Mindboggle-101. Not reviewed by a clinician.

## In-app display

The viewer shows an "Attributions" control that reproduces the above sources
and licences, satisfying the CC BY 4.0 attribution requirement for the brain
mesh.
